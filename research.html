  • Learning Where the Guarantee Ends: Failure-Localized Neural Augmentation of Approximation Algorithms
    October, 2026
    Studies how to structure the learning problem when machine learning is applied to combinatorial optimization. The theory behind an approximation algorithm identifies the states where its decisions may be suboptimal, and the model learns only those decisions. Case study: the Steiner tree problem with edge lengths 1 and 2.
